Pour & Freeze: Ice Puzzle

Privacy Policy and Support · a.k.soft games

Pour & Freeze: Ice Puzzle collects no data at all. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. It works offline.

This page covers Pour & Freeze: Ice Puzzle (the "Game") for iPhone, published on the App Store by Andrii Kulyk as a.k.soft games ("we", "us"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. What the Game collects

Nothing. The Game does not collect, send or share:

  • names, email addresses or any account information (there are no accounts)
  • advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any tracking data
  • location, contacts, photos or files
  • usage statistics or crash reports of its own

Your progress (levels opened, stars) and the sound setting are saved only on your device. Deleting the Game deletes them. The Game makes no network requests.

Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (such as downloads) from users who have agreed to share them with developers; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13, and it has no ads, purchases, chat or links out of the Game.

3. Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, we will update this page before it does, and the date below.

4. Support and contact

Questions, ideas, a bug, or a level you could not beat? Write to kyluk5@gmail.com. We read everything.
